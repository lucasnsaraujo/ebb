# Third-party material

## Simple Icons

The provider logos in the accounts list (Claude, Google Gemini, Kimi, Meta and Qwen) come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) 16.36.0, released under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). Their path data lives in `Sources/EbbCore/ProviderMark.swift`, rewritten to absolute commands but otherwise unchanged. Ebb draws them in one colour, at the size of the text around them.

Provider names and logos are trademarks of their owners, used only to identify which service an account belongs to.

Simple Icons has no logo for Codex, Devin, Antigravity, Vertex AI, AI Studio, iFlow or Grok, so those get a two-letter tile that Ebb draws itself.
